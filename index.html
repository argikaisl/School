Who we are

Bringing Europe's video games ecosystem together.

ISFE represents the video games industry in Europe. Our network brings together national trade associations across Europe and leading video game companies, creating a shared voice for the sector.

We work with policymakers and stakeholders to support an environment where creativity, investment, innovation and responsible gaming can flourish.

The industry

More than entertainment. A creative force.

Video games combine art, technology, storytelling and entrepreneurship. Across Europe, studios, publishers and technology partners contribute to a dynamic digital and cultural landscape.

ISFE works to ensure that the industry's voice is heard and its potential is understood.
